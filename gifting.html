Questions

Frequently asked

Do you sell products directly?
No. India Selection is a curation and sourcing concierge, not a shop. We connect you with the brand or maker; you order with them directly.
What is the minimum order quantity?
It depends on the maker and category. Many support MOQs from a few hundred units; some premium items need more. Tell us your volume and we will match accordingly.
Can you do custom branding or hampers?
Often yes. Several makers offer white-label and custom packaging. Share your idea in the notes and we will shortlist partners who can deliver it.
Is there a fee to use this channel?
The enquiry is free. We earn only if you place an order with a maker we introduce — there is no charge for the shortlist or introduction.
