What are the best TWS earbuds brands in India in 2026?
boAt leads by volume, with Noise, Boult, Mivi and pTron as strong Indian challengers; Realme, OnePlus and JBL add feature depth. See our Electronics rankings for the full TWS, smartwatch and smartphone lists.
Which Indian electronics brands are Make-in-India?
boAt, Noise, Fire-Boltt, pTron, Lava and Micromax are Indian-origin brands; Lava and Micromax additionally design and manufacture smartphones in India.
Which is the best budget smartphone brand in India?
Under ₹15,000, Xiaomi/Redmi and Realme lead on specs; POCO on performance; Lava and Micromax for Make-in-India buyers; Samsung Galaxy M/F and Motorola for clean software and support.
