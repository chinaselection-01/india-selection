Questions

Frequently asked

What is India Selection?
India Selection is an editorial curation platform that showcases curated Indian brands and products — starting with insulated stainless-steel drinkware. We research, rank and present trusted Indian makers so global buyers can discover them.
Do you sell products directly?
No. India Selection is a discovery and curation site, not a shop. Each brand links to its official store, and sourcing enquiries go to our partner maker, Unifly.
How do you rank brands?
We rank by market presence, price accessibility, manufacturing capability, certifications (such as BIS/ISI) and D2C momentum. Rankings are editorial and updated periodically — always verify prices and certifications with the maker.
Which Indian bottle brand is best?
For overall leadership, Milton and Cello dominate; for certified value, Pexpo; for premium BIS-certified and private-label sourcing, HYV; for wholesale/OEM, Nirlon and Unifly. See our Rankings page for the full picture.
