Questions

Frequently asked

What are the most popular packaged food brands in India?
Cadbury, Amul, Nestlé, Parle, ITC, Haldiram's, Britannia, Tata Tea and Lay's lead their categories — chocolates, biscuits, snacks, tea and noodles. See our Packaged Food rankings for the full brand-by-brand breakdown.
Which Indian chocolate brand is the best?
Cadbury leads on everyday and gifting chocolate (Dairy Milk, Silk); Amul is the desi dairy-chocolate favourite; Nestlé owns impulse (KitKat, Munch); ITC's Dark Fantasy covers premium.
Which instant noodle brand do Indians prefer?
Maggi is the default by a wide margin for its masala taste and 2-minute habit; ITC Yippee! is the strong #2, with Wai Wai (eat-raw) and Patanjali (atta) as popular alternatives.
Where can I source Indian packaged food for import or wholesale?
Most brands here run export and distributor networks (Haldiram's, Britannia, ITC, Tata, Amul among them). For private label or bulk sourcing, use the 'List your brand' link to reach our sourcing network.
