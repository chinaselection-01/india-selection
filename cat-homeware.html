Questions

Frequently asked

What are the best pressure cooker brands in India in 2026?
Hawkins leads on durability and safety, Prestige on range and service, Pigeon on budget; Butterfly and Vinod are strong regional/premium picks. See our Homeware rankings for cookware and storage lists too.
Which Indian cookware brand is best for induction?
Look for induction-compatible bases: Hawkins (Contura), Prestige, Pigeon, Vinod (triply) and Bergner all offer induction-friendly stainless and hard-anodised lines.
Which brand makes the best storage containers in India?
Signoraware and Nayasa lead on plastic storage with tight seals and huge SKU ranges; Cello and Milton cover steel-and-plastic; Tupperware remains the reusable-ware legacy name.
Where can I source kitchenware wholesale from India?
Hawkins, Prestige, Vinod and the plastic-storage makers all supply distributors and export. For private label or bulk, use the 'List your brand' link to reach our sourcing network.
