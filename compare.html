Frequently asked

What are these India Selection brand guides?
Four editorial lenses on the same 55 Indian brands: alternatives to well-known brands, the best value picks, the most premium names, and light-luxury finds. Each guide links to full brand profiles.
How are brands placed in each guide?
Editorially, on market presence, manufacturing capability, certifications, price positioning and D2C momentum. Guides are unpaid and updated periodically — always verify prices with the maker.
Does India Selection sell these products?
No. We are a curation and discovery site, not a shop. Each brand links to its official store, and bulk sourcing enquiries go to our partner maker, Unifly.
