About us

What India Selection is

India Selection is an editorial showcase — a curated, easy-to-browse directory of trusted Indian consumer brands across home appliances, consumer electronics, homeware, bedding, packaged food and drinkware. Think of it as a newspaper for Indian brands: we present the products, the context, and the real market picture so visitors can discover what's worth knowing.

We don't sell here

This is a showcase, not a shop. We do not process orders or payments on the site. If a visitor wants to actually source a product, they reach out by email and we connect them with manufacturers — including direct sourcing from China at factory pricing.

How we make money

The site runs contextual advertising (Google AdSense) and may feature sponsored brand placements. We are independent: a brand's presence in our rankings is editorial, not a purchase. Always verify prices, MOQ and certifications with the maker before placing any order.

Editorial independence

Brand profiles and rankings are compiled from publicly available information for reference. We are not affiliated with most brands listed, and we do not guarantee the accuracy of third-party data. Content is provided "as is".
