Frequently asked

What is light luxury in Indian brands?
Light (affordable) luxury means a premium feel — design, finish, materials — at a price that stays mainstream. Think Borosil Hydra, Vaya, The Sleep Company or a boAt flagship.
Which Indian brands are good affordable-luxury gifts?
Drinkware is the easiest: Vaya and HYV support custom gifting, and Borosil reads premium. For bulk gifting, use our Corporate Gifting guide.
How is light luxury different from premium?
Premium is the top tier — paid for outright. Light luxury gets you most of the feel for much less. Compare this page with our Most Premium guide.
