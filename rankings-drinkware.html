Best Indian Drinkware brands

Drinkware

Top 11 Indian Drinkware brands

Ranked by market presence, manufacturing capability, certifications and D2C momentum. Editorial and updated periodically — verify prices with makers.

1

Milton

Profile →
Hamilton Housewares, Mumbai, founded in 1972. Milton's Thermosteel is the generic name for insulated bottles in India — sold in 80+ countries with about 16.5% of the organised market (Ken Research). The bottle most Indian homes already own.
2

Cello

Profile →
Cello World, Mumbai, runs 13 plants, 700+ distributors and 50,000 retail touchpoints across 65+ countries. It dominates the under-₹800 tier with honest pricing and a basic-but-dependable build.
3

Borosil

Profile →
Mumbai, founded 1962, Borosil grew from borosilicate lab glass into householdware. Its Hydra steel line (₹600–1,500) brings a strong warranty and holds 40–50% of India's organised glass-bottle segment.
4

Tupperware India

Profile →
Best known globally for plastic food storage, Tupperware is also a long-standing player in reusable and insulated flasks through direct-sales channels. Strong brand trust, with a lighter insulated-steel focus than the top three.
5

Pexpo

Profile →
Based in Sonipat, Haryana, Pexpo sells under the Go24 and Perch labels. Its ISI-marked bottles carry up to a 24-month warranty (Bravo at ~₹771) — the cheapest bottle with a credible certification, and a real challenger in the value-insulated tier.
6

HYV

Profile →
HYV is India-made with 50,000+ units/month capacity and full-range BIS IS 17526:2021 certification. It takes branded orders from a 25-unit MOQ and custom colourways from 1,000 — a credible private-label partner and a rising D2C name.
7

Vaya

Profile →
Vaya is an established mid-premium Indian brand with a wider product range and a gifting focus. Known for clean design and reliable thermal performance across bottles, flasks and lunch solutions.
8

InstaCuppa

Profile →
InstaCuppa, Tirupati, since 2016, makes a one-hand sports sipper (400g body) with a 10-day trial. At similar price points it offers wider mouths than the legacy brands — a practical D2C pick for gym and travel.
9

SOLARA

Profile →
SOLARA, Hyderabad, sells motivational and personalised bottles and tumblers in 18/8 steel, shipping with the most caps and straws in the box. A D2C brand built on customisation and social appeal.
10

Nirlon Kitchenware

Profile →
Nirlon Kitchenware, Palghar, Maharashtra, is a long-standing vacuum-insulated bottle manufacturer (Regal Touch, Loki lines) in 304-grade steel, with ex-works pricing from ~₹125–475/pc. A representative of India's organised manufacturing tail.
11

Unifly Private Limited

Profile →
Founded in 2020 and based in New Delhi, Unifly makes double-wall stainless-steel water bottles, vacuum flasks and sippers. Part of a group with six manufacturing verticals and a drinkware capacity of 500,000–600,000 units a month. We selected them as our launch maker for consistent quality and real factory capability.
