Questions

Frequently asked

What are the best mattress brands in India in 2026?
Duroflex tops 2026 rankings for orthopedic support; Wakefit and The Sleep Company lead on D2C value and sleep-tech; Sleepwell and Kurlon remain the trusted legacy choices. See our Bedding rankings for the full lists.
Which mattress brand is best for back pain in India?
Duroflex and The Sleep Company (SmartGRID) are frequently recommended for spinal support; Kurlon and Sleepwell offer firm coir/foam options. Always match firmness to your sleep position and body weight.
Which Indian bedsheet brands are best?
Spaces (Welspun) and Bombay Dyeing lead on premium cotton quality and trust; Raymond Home and Trident add export-grade cotton; Swayam and Story@Home cover design-led and value ends.
Where can I source home textiles / mattresses from India?
India is a global textile hub — Welspun, Trident and Bombay Dyeing all export. For bulk or private-label bedding, use the 'List your brand' link to reach our sourcing network.
