Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

India Selection is an editorial showcase of Indian consumer brands. We aim to be transparent about the limited data we collect. This policy explains what we gather, why, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

a) Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregated, anonymous traffic — which pages are visited, approximate country, device type, and referral source. This helps us improve the site. GA4 sets first-party cookies and collects your IP address (truncated), which is processed by Google.

b) Advertising cookies

We participate in Google AdSense to display ads. AdSense and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. These are third-party cookies set by Google and its advertising partners.

c) Emails you send us

If you contact us by email (for example, to request sourcing from China), we receive the address and content you provide. We use it only to reply and to facilitate your request. We do not sell or share your email with unrelated third parties.

2. Your choices

  • You can disable advertising and analytics cookies via your browser settings or Google's Ads Settings.
  • Blocking cookies does not prevent you from browsing the site.

3. Third parties

Google Analytics and Google AdSense are operated by Google LLC. Their data handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control those services beyond standard configuration.

Questions about this policy? Email us at bob@india-selection.com.
