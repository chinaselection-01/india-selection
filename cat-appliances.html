Questions

Frequently asked

What are the best home-appliance brands in India in 2026?
For mixer grinders, Philips, Bajaj, Prestige, Preethi and Butterfly lead; for ceiling fans, Atomberg, Crompton, Orient and Havells; for washing machines, LG, Samsung, IFB and Godrej. See our Appliances rankings for the full lists.
Which Indian appliance brands offer the best value?
Bajaj and Crompton cover the affordable, service-heavy tier, while Atomberg's BLDC fans cut electricity bills over time. Prestige and Havells sit at the premium, design-led end.
Are there Make-in-India appliance brands here?
Yes — Atomberg (BLDC fans), Bajaj, Crompton, Havells and Voltas are all Indian-origin or Indian-led, with local manufacturing and service networks.
Where can I source home appliances wholesale from India?
Most major brands sell via distributors and marketplaces rather than direct OEM. For private-label or contract manufacturing, talk to our partner maker network — use the 'List your brand' link to start a sourcing conversation.
